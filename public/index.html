Each client has its own apps and its own databases. This page shows where each engagement stands: the stage, open actions and what comes next. It holds no client content.

What the suite covers

  • Australia and Singapore in full, five main sectors each. Australia: superannuation, banking, insurance, health and critical infrastructure. Singapore: banking, insurance, capital markets, listed companies and critical infrastructure. Each main sector has its own pack and worked examples.
  • Best-practice baselines for any jurisdiction. Every client's apps carry eight baseline packs drawn from international standard setters: banking; insurance; securities and investment advice; health care; payments and money transmission; critical infrastructure, run by government or by a company; and government in general. A client in a sector with no pack of its own is offered its baseline at set-up. A baseline names no regulator; the client's own obligations come from the matter brief.
  • Public-body clients. For government and government-run infrastructure, the apps name the accountable authority where they would otherwise say board, on screen and in every download.
  • Four controls in every baseline. Agent controls on every AI use at A3 and above (AI Use Assessment); a fast path the board sets for low-risk uses (AI Board Direction, step B4); one record of each provider's evidence, offered against every regime in the matter brief (AI Use Assessment); and two productivity measures (BM7 and BM8 in AI Board Direction, M11 and M12 in AI Risk Appetite).
  • A client in another jurisdiction. The method for adding a jurisdiction is published with the suite. Run it once and provision as usual: every client the practice provisions afterwards gets it.
